# Warhammer 40,000 11th Edition dataset

Unofficial, fan-maintained data for personal and community use. Warhammer 40,000 and all associated names, rules and points values are the intellectual property of Games Workshop. This dataset is not affiliated with, licensed by or endorsed by Games Workshop.

## Coverage in 1.5.8.0

- Genestealer Cults: all 24 native datasheets, plus the Tectonic Fragdrill as a Warhammer Legends unit. Switch on Legends for a list to select it.
- Invulnerable saves are given as an Inv characteristic and as an ability on each unit that has one.
- Astra Militarum: a full faction. The 51 non-AIRCRAFT datasheets available to Genestealer Cults as Brood Brother Auxilia allies are checked against Wahapedia. The other 21 datasheets, 61 Legends, 11 detachments, 34 enhancements and the Voice of Command army rule are an unchecked BSData draft, with points for the Hippogriff AFV and the Legends weapon batteries taken from Wahapedia. Hell's Last is left out because it has no 11th Edition datasheet.
- Tyranids: a full faction. The 14 Vanguard Invader datasheets used as Final Day allies are checked against Wahapedia: Winged Tyranid Prime, Gargoyles, Raveners, Hyperadapted Raveners, Lictor, Neurolictor, Von Ryan's Leapers, Mawloc, Trygon, Tyrannocyte, Winged Hive Tyrant, Deathleaper, The Red Terror and Parasite of Mortrex. Deathleaper cannot be your Warlord. The one-per-army limit for Epic Heroes is not enforced. The other 38 datasheets, 5 Legends, 10 detachments, 34 enhancements and the army rules are an unchecked BSData draft, with points for Carnifexes and Mucolid Spores taken from Wahapedia. The Winged Tyranid Prime (Alpha Warrior) and Hyperadapted Raveners (Alpha Invader) give [SUSTAINED HITS 1] to the unit they lead.
- Unit and weapon profiles, model compositions, abilities and points tiers.
- Duplicate-unit prices are represented where the first/second copies and third copy have different costs.
- Datasheets are grouped as Characters, Battleline, Dedicated Transports and Other Datasheets, and allies under Allied Units.
- Acolyte Hybrids with Hand Flamers and with Autopistols enforce their model count and per-unit/per-five-model wargear limits.
- Hybrid Metamorphs enforce their model count, hand flamer replacements and single cult icon.
- Atalan Jackals enforce 4-8 Jackals, 1-2 Wolfquads and their per-four-Jackal weapon limits. The rule that 2 Wolfquads require 8 Jackals cannot be enforced; build these units to the datasheet.
- Neophyte Hybrids enforce their 10 or 20 model sizes, leader weapon swap and up to two heavy and two special weapons per ten models. The one-of-each-weapon-per-ten limit cannot be enforced; build these units to the datasheet.
- The Patriarch carries the must-be-Warlord flag, but WarOrgan does not currently enforce it. Select it as Warlord yourself.
- Biophagus, Clamavus, Locus and Nexos attach as Support characters alongside a Leader.
- Leader effects are applied to the led unit: Might From Beyond, Twisted Science, Bio-horror Disruption, Sudden Assault and Nexus of Devotion. Cult Demagogue (+1 to Hit), The Chosen One and the Locus's Bodyguard ability are shown as text only.

- The Cult Ambush army rule and all nine detachments: Heroes of the Uprising, Purestrain Broodswarm, Xenocult Masses, Final Day, Host of Ascension, Xenocreed Congregation, Outlander Claw, Biosanctic Broodsurge and Brood Brother Auxilia, each with its rule, Force Disposition, enhancements and stratagems. Heroes of the Uprising adds KILLER to the Kelermorph, Locus, Reductus Saboteur and Sanctus. Enhancements that change profiles adjust them; Upgrades (Mark of the Star Children, Devious Disguises) can go on non-CHARACTER units. WarOrgan does not enforce the limit of three copies of the same Upgrade; build to the core rules. Host of Ascension costs 3 DP, so WarOrgan cannot offer it in Incursion, although the core rules let an Incursion army take a single 3 DP detachment.
- The ten 11th Edition core stratagems, including Explosives and Crushing Impact (which replace Grenade and Tank Shock).
- Battle sizes use the 11th Edition detachment points and enhancement limits: Incursion 2 DP and 2 enhancements, Strike Force 3 DP and 4. Onslaught is not in the 11th Edition core rules, so it uses the Strike Force limits. Unit limits are not enforced.

Final Day lets you include Tyranids Vanguard Invader units (excluding AIRCRAFT, BROODLORD and GENESTEALERS) up to half your points. They appear under Allied Units and cannot be your Warlord.

Brood Brother Auxilia lets you include the 51 eligible Astra Militarum datasheets (none with AIRCRAFT, COMMISSAR, EPIC HERO, MILITARUM TEMPESTUS, OGRYN, RATLING, TECH-PRIEST ENGINSEER or MINISTORUM PRIEST) up to half your points. They appear under Allied Units, cannot be your Warlord and lose Voice of Command.

## Draft factions (unchecked)

These 31 factions are drafts converted automatically from the November 2026 BSData dump. They have not been checked against Wahapedia, GW's faction packs or the codexes, so expect mistakes in rules text, wargear options and points. Treat them as a starting point and build lists to the datasheets.

- Most draft factions have no stratagems yet. A few detachments have no enhancements because BSData has none for them. The 16 Space Marine detachment rosters include 52 Wahapedia stratagems. Enhancements, detachment rules and leader abilities are text only: the app does not apply their effects.
- Space Marine chapters are separate factions. Each can also take the Space Marines datasheets and has the generic Space Marine detachments alongside its own.
- Where BSData's wargear could not be converted exactly, weapons are offered as swaps for a starting weapon of the same type, so some units allow loadouts their datasheet does not. Build units to the datasheet.
- BSData datasheets that no longer exist in 11th Edition (Tactical Squad, Captain in Gravis Armour, Furioso Dreadnought, older Death Company, Deathwing Command Squad and Wolf Guard variants) are left out.

## Installation

In WarOrgan's dataset importer, use:

`https://github.com/DominicWatts/WarOrgan.Data/releases/latest/download/DominicWatts-WH40K11e.zip`

The URL always targets the most recent published release. The downloaded ZIP should be imported directly and not extracted.

- Wahapedia 11th Edition pages: Genestealer Cults datasheets, the Tyranid and Astra Militarum allied datasheets, and the faction pages used to correct individual draft points, datasheets and rules. Checked 23-24 November 2026.
- Games Workshop Genestealer Cults Faction Pack, version 1.0, legal for matched play from 20 June 2026, and Tyranids Faction Pack, version 1.3, legal from 14 October 2026. Checked 23 November 2026.
- Games Workshop Warhammer Community faction packs and Munitorum Field Manual are the canonical authority for published changes and points.

Community datasets are used as structured authoring aids, not as unquestioned authorities. Values should be rechecked following every Games Workshop balance or faction update.

## Known limitations

- The WarOrgan builder validates schema and internal references; it cannot prove that game data matches the current rules.
- Wargear limits are being audited unit by unit. Users should consult the current faction rules for units not yet called out as constraint-verified in the coverage notes.
- The draft factions are converted automatically and unchecked: expect mistakes in rules text, wargear options and points until each faction has been checked against Wahapedia.
- Stable unit IDs must not be renamed after release because saved army lists refer to them.
