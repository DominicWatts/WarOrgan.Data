# Dataset changelog

2026-11-23 : 1.2.1.0 - Added the Purestrain Broodswarm and Xenocult Masses detachments and Force Dispositions for all three detachments.

2026-11-23 : 1.2.0.0 - Added the Cult Ambush army rule, the Heroes of the Uprising detachment and 11th Edition detachment points and enhancement limits.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.12.0 - Moved the Tectonic Fragdrill to a Warhammer Legends roster and checked the datasheets against the GSC Faction Pack 1.0.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.11.0 - Checked Reductus Saboteur, Sanctus and Tectonic Fragdrill, completing the check of all 25 Genestealer Cults datasheets; vehicle bases shown as Hull.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.10.0 - Checked Nexos, Patriarch, Primus and Purestrain Genestealers against the current datasheets and corrected minor wording.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.9.0 - Checked Goliath Truck, Jackal Alphus, Kelermorph, Locus and Magus; fixed the Jackal Alphus save to 5+ and renamed the Magus weapon to Magus stave.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.8.0 - Support characters now attach alongside Leaders, and the Biophagus, Benefictus, Locus and Iconward leader effects now apply to the led unit; Might From Beyond now includes the Patriarch.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.7.0 - Checked Atalan Jackals, Benefictus, Biophagus, Clamavus and Goliath Rockgrinder; Biophagus can now support Acolyte Hybrids with Hand Flamers.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.6.0 - Checked Aberrants, Abominant, Achilles Ridgerunners, Acolyte Hybrids with Hand Flamers and Acolyte Iconward against the current datasheets and corrected ability wording and the demolition charges name.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.5.0 - Ridgerunners and Rockgrinder now start with a heavy mining laser and swap it rather than adding weapons; Ridgerunner heavy mining lasers cost 10 points; Goliath Truck melee weapon renamed to Armoured hull.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.4.0 - Removed the arrow prefix from dual-profile weapon names.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.3.0 - Neophyte Hybrids heavy and special weapons are now two selection groups of up to two per ten models.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.2.0 - Rebuilt Neophyte Hybrids, Acolyte Hybrids with Autopistols, Hybrid Metamorphs and Atalan Jackals unit sizes and wargear limits; noted that WarOrgan does not enforce the Patriarch's Warlord rule.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.1.0 - Grouped GSC datasheets by category, shortened base sizes, enforced Acolyte Hybrids with Hand Flamers wargear limits and added the Patriarch's Might From Beyond effect.

2026-11-23 : 1.1.0.0 - Initial Genestealer Cults dataset with all 25 datasheets.
